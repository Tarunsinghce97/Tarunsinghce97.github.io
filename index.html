About

Researching how materials deform.

My research focuses on constitutive modelling of rate-dependent materials undergoing finite deformation. It has progressed from formulating finite-deformation rheological connections, to developing thermodynamically consistent viscoelastic models, and then to deriving scalar, deformation-mode-specific constitutive equations for material characterization. Current directions include finite-element implementation and extensions to viscoplasticity.

Research programme

Constitutive mechanics at finite deformation

My research is concerned with the formulation, analysis and computational implementation of constitutive models for rate-dependent materials undergoing finite deformations. The work connects continuum mechanics, rheological modelling, thermodynamics and material characterization.

01 · FRAMEWORK

Rheological connections

How to represent series and parallel connections at finite deformation.

02 · CONSTITUTIVE MODELS

Finite viscoelasticity

Thermodynamically consistent models using evolving natural configurations.

03 · CHARACTERIZATION

Mode-specific equations

Scalar conjugate pairs and analytical solutions for selected deformation modes.

RESEARCH THEME 01

Rheological connections at finite deformation

Extended series and parallel rheological connections to finite-deformation mechanics using multiple natural configurations. The framework characterizes the connections through stress-power distribution, providing a strategy for formulating rheological networks beyond the conventional small-strain setting.

Related work · IJES (2025)
RESEARCH THEME 02

Thermodynamically consistent finite viscoelasticity

Developed finite-deformation viscoelastic constitutive models within the theory of evolving natural configurations. The work treats Maxwell, Zener, Poynting–Thomson and Kelvin–Voigt-type models, with particular attention to the driving/configurational force associated with parallel rheological structures.

Related work · IJSS (2026)
RESEARCH THEME 03

QR kinematics and scalar constitutive formulation

Introduced a deformation-mode-specific framework based on scalar, conjugate stress–strain pairs. A QR-based decomposition is used to obtain scalar governing equations for selected loading modes, supporting analytical solutions and physically meaningful parameter identification.

Related work · JMPS (2026)
RESEARCH THEME 04

Anisotropic viscoelastic constitutive modelling

Derived constitutive equations for anisotropic material responses as part of the finite-deformation viscoelasticity work, extending the formulation beyond isotropic material behaviour.

Published formulation · IJSS (2026)
Ongoing directions

From constitutive theory to computation

Current work focuses on translating constitutive developments into computational tools and extending the modelling framework to additional rate-dependent material behaviour.

ONGOING · COMPUTATIONAL MECHANICS

Finite-element implementation

Implementing constitutive models in Abaqus user material subroutines (UMAT/VUMAT) and FEniCSx, with the aim of solving boundary-value problems and large-deformation simulations.

ONGOING · MATERIAL MODELLING

Extension to viscoplasticity

Extending the constitutive modelling framework to describe viscoplastic material behaviour under finite deformation.
