About

Researching how materials deform.

My research focuses on continuum-mechanical descriptions of complex material behavior at finite deformation. I am interested in physically motivated constitutive frameworks, rheological models, parameter identification and computational implementation for nonlinear material response.

Research

What I work on

01

Finite-strain viscoelasticity

Constitutive descriptions of rate-dependent materials under large deformation using nonlinear continuum mechanics and internal natural configurations.

02

Rheological connections

Extension of series and parallel rheological concepts from one-dimensional models to finite-deformation frameworks with multiple natural configurations.

03

Constitutive modeling

Development and calibration of material models using experimental data across deformation modes and loading rates.

04

Computational implementation

Numerical integration and finite-element implementation of nonlinear constitutive equations for large-deformation simulations.
